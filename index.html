The "Sweet Spot" Strategy

Most applicants target Istanbul and Ankara, creating massive competition. Your goal is to find universities with High Academic Quality but Medium Competition.

Why This Approach?

  • ➤

    The Istanbul Trap: Universities like Boğaziçi or ITU receive thousands of applications for few spots.

  • ➤

    The Anatolian Advantage: Cities like Konya, Kayseri, and Samsun have huge, well-funded campuses but fewer international applicants.

  • ➤

    Strategic Neighbors: Sakarya and Kocaeli are close to Istanbul but technically outside, offering a "best of both worlds" scenario.
